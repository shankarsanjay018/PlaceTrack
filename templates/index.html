THE CHALLENGE

Placement preparation becomes difficult when everything is scattered.

Students often manage applications, interview updates, company information and placement progress across different places. PlaceTrack brings these activities together into one organized workspace.

01

Too many applications

When multiple companies and roles are involved, it becomes difficult to remember where each application stands.

02

Status updates get missed

Applications can move from Applied to Assessment or Interview without having a clear place to track those changes.

03

Opportunities are difficult to organize

Internship and placement opportunities contain important details such as eligibility, skills, location and deadlines.

04

Progress is difficult to understand

Without a central view, it is harder to understand application activity, interview progress and final outcomes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about the PlaceTrack platform and how its student and admin workflows work.

What is PlaceTrack?

PlaceTrack is a student placement management system for organizing applications, opportunities, analytics and notifications.

Can students manage multiple applications?

Yes. Students can maintain multiple application records and monitor the status of each one.

What can administrators manage?

Administrators can manage opportunities, review applications and update application statuses.

How do application notifications work?

When an administrator changes an application's status, PlaceTrack creates a notification for the related student.
